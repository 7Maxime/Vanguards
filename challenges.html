Our E-Lab journey — Challenges 1 to 6

Each challenge developed a different part of our learning journey. Together, they helped us build self-awareness, explore community realities, engage people directly, conduct research, launch our mission, and finally document everything through this website.

1

Challenge One — Introduction

This challenge introduced who we are as individuals and as a Think Tank. We created elevator pitches and began presenting our strengths, interests, and identity as a team.

  • Introduced ourselves and our personal strengths
  • Presented elevator pitches
  • Started shaping our Think Tank identity
Elevator pitch Identity Self-presentation
2

Challenge Two — Discover Africa

We explored Bigogwe and reflected on the place, its activities, local experiences, and some misconceptions people may hold, especially concerning herdsmen and the realities of the community.

  • Visited and observed Bigogwe
  • Documented activities and experiences
  • Reflected on misconceptions and deeper understanding
Bigogwe Field discovery Cultural insight
3

Challenge Three — Help-LAB

In this challenge, we practiced empathy and service. We visited a single mother with three children and supported her with food supplies and cleaning equipment while documenting the visit and the experience.

  • Visited a vulnerable household
  • Provided food and cleaning materials
  • Reflected on compassion, dignity, and social responsibility
Community service Empathy Impact
4

Challenge Four — Hunt for Treasure

We interviewed AgriWin Ltd to understand how a real agribusiness operates, what challenge it addresses, its daily operations, its growth story, and what lessons we could apply to our own Think Tank work.

  • Researched AgriWin Ltd and its agriculture-focused work
  • Conducted and documented an interview
  • Learned about nursery production, extension services, and market-oriented agriculture
Company research Interview AgriWin Ltd
5

Challenge Five — Mission Launch

Here we transformed our research into a pitch proposal. We presented our mission, problem, solution, budget, prototype, and expected impact as a grant-ready concept focused on Bugesera youth and agribusiness.

  • Created an investor-style pitch proposal
  • Presented the Youth Agribusiness Training & Incubation Program
  • Connected problem, evidence, and impact in one coherent solution
Open pitch presentation
Pitch deck Mission launch Prototype
